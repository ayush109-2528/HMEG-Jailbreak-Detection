## Remaining external setup and research limitations

- This verifies the local site, not a new production deployment. The existing Vercel site was not changed.
- The hosted worker/container configuration is prepared, but its hosting provider, credentials, public API URL, HTTPS ingress, and shared persistent model volume still need provisioning. Docker deployment itself was not executed here.
- The installed model is a four-expert configuration. The optional Meaning encoder is not installed. Its absent score is never fabricated.
- Initial fitting uses 20,000 training rows, 1,000 validation rows, and 1,000 held-out test rows after overlap checks, rather than all 300,000 rows. The predominantly synthetic corpus limits interpretation of the measured results.
- Single-guardrail baseline and OOD result sections have no recorded experiments yet; they clearly show empty states. Llama Guard and NeMo are literature-only comparisons.
- Training jobs are operator-triggered. Visitor predictions do not become training labels automatically. Public model training and editing endpoints are intentionally absent.
- One non-failing upstream TestClient/httpx deprecation warning remains in backend test output.
